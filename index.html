WHO WE ARE

Every community deserves the chance to thrive.

TriArc Community Initiative (TACI) is a community-focused organisation committed to empowering people, strengthening local capacity and supporting practical solutions to social and environmental challenges.

Our approach values dignity, participation, fairness and partnerships. We aim to work with community members and other stakeholders to turn shared priorities into meaningful action.

WHAT WE DO

Our five programmes

Connected priorities. Community-centred solutions.

About the Programme

TACI’s Girl-Child Empowerment Programme seeks to support girls to realise their potential through education, life skills, confidence-building and meaningful participation in community development.

Key objectives

  • Promote access to education and learning opportunities for girls.
  • Build confidence, leadership and life skills.
  • Raise awareness about girls’ rights, safety and wellbeing.
  • Encourage equal opportunities and community support for girls.

Planned activities

Educational awareness sessions, mentorship, leadership training, life-skills activities and community dialogues.

Expected impact

Girls who are better supported, informed, confident and able to participate in shaping their futures.

About the Programme

TACI’s Disability Empowerment Programme promotes the rights, dignity, inclusion and participation of persons with disabilities in education, livelihoods and community life.

Key objectives

  • Promote equal access to community services and opportunities.
  • Increase awareness about disability rights and inclusion.
  • Encourage participation in education, skills development and livelihoods.
  • Support communities to identify and reduce barriers to accessibility.

Planned activities

Community sensitisation, inclusive learning activities, skills training, advocacy and engagement with disability groups.

Expected impact

More inclusive communities where persons with disabilities can participate with dignity and equal opportunity.

About the Programme

TACI’s WASH Programme works to improve community awareness and practices relating to safe water, sanitation and personal and environmental hygiene.

Key objectives

  • Promote safe water handling, storage and responsible use.
  • Encourage proper sanitation and waste disposal.
  • Strengthen handwashing and other healthy hygiene practices.
  • Support school and community WASH awareness.

Planned activities

Hygiene education, handwashing demonstrations, sanitation awareness, clean-up activities and community engagement on water safety.

Expected impact

Improved hygiene awareness and community practices that contribute to healthier living environments.

About the Programme

TACI’s Agriculture and Food Security Programme aims to strengthen household food production, promote sustainable farming and improve community resilience and livelihoods.

Key objectives

  • Promote sustainable and climate-smart farming practices.
  • Encourage household food production and nutrition awareness.
  • Support agricultural skills and knowledge sharing.
  • Promote practical livelihood opportunities for vulnerable households.

Planned activities

Farmer awareness sessions, demonstration gardens, food production training, nutrition education and community agricultural initiatives.

Expected impact

Improved agricultural knowledge, stronger household food security and more sustainable livelihoods.

About the Programme

TACI’s Environmental Conservation and Climate Resilience Programme promotes responsible environmental management, restoration of natural resources and community action to address climate-related challenges.

Key objectives

  • Promote tree planting and protection of existing trees.
  • Increase awareness of climate change and environmental protection.
  • Encourage responsible waste management and reduced pollution.
  • Support sustainable community practices and resilience.

Planned activities

Tree-planting campaigns, environmental education, community clean-ups, waste-management awareness and local conservation activities.

Expected impact

Communities with stronger environmental awareness and increased participation in conservation and climate resilience.
